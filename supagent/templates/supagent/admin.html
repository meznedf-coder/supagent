Chat settings

LLM

An OpenAI-compatible chat API with tool calls. Authentication: none, a fixed access token, or a token from the company middleware (OAuth2 client credentials with the consumer key and secret, and a client certificate when the middleware asks for one), renewed before it expires.

Daily learning

Every day at the chosen hour, the metrics (promagg) and the indices (osagg) are read as the learning user: types, units, values, series, time ranges, relations, and what changed since the day before. The runs are listed below, each with its steps (when, how long, what it counted) and what it asked of the LLM: the daily learning, the Context builds and the classifications (Classify now, or superset supagent classify: the categories only, which the daily learning also does at its end).

Backups of the knowledge

One file a day holds the whole knowledge as it is: the categories with the System map, the catalog and its versions, the team memory, the documents, the notes, the Context, the learned answers and paths, the descriptions of the data, the settings (never a secret; not the chats). The last ones are kept: the history to go back to. Restore puts back the parts you choose as they were in a backup, in place of the present ones, after saving the present state in a backup of its own, so a restore can be undone. The files are on the server, in the directory below; with several servers, give them a directory they share. Download keeps one elsewhere. Without the workers' beat, plan superset supagent backup yourself.

Knowledge search and memory

With each question the agent gets the knowledge that looks relevant: the dictionary, the catalog's guides and rules, the team's learned answers and memory, the documents and sites. Words are searched with PostgreSQL's full-text search; with an embedding model (e.g. bge-m3 on the same gateway), meanings too. With pgvector, pg_textsearch or pg_trgm in Superset's database, everything is also kept in the knowledge store (schema supagent_store).
